# Forever Relationships

See your history with everyone you play with in World of Warcraft: Forever: time together, dungeons cleared, help given. Made only from facts the game recorded; no ratings, no notes, no guesses.

Type **/fr** in game to open it. On first login, a welcome screen lets you choose which optional layers to turn on. Everything starts off.

- **`/fr feedback`: found a bug or have an idea? Tell us right here in game. It's the only feedback channel.**
- `/fr player <name>`: your relationship with a player (or right-click them → Relationship)
- `/fr privacy`: the optional cloud layers, the companion app and your data
- `/fr help`: every command

**Privacy:** chat text is never stored, and nothing leaves your computer unless you turn a layer on. Policy: https://forever.gameplaybr.com.br/privacy

Companion app and source code: https://github.com/viniciuscotrim/forever-relationships
