Privacy policy

Forever Relationships, an unofficial addon for World of Warcraft: Forever. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. Last updated 2026-11-04.

What Forever Relationships is

It shows you how you've related to the people you play with: time together, content completed, kills, trades, help, quests. It only uses data the game itself produces while you play together. It never reads chat. It never records opinions. It never scores or infers anything about anyone.

Who is responsible

The developer of Forever Relationships is the data controller. Contact: the in-game channel /fr feedback, or the self-service page below.

What is never collected

Chat text or message counts. Notes, ratings or opinions about anyone. Inferences about anyone's behavior. Payment data.

Recipients

We sell nothing and share nothing. Data is hosted on a server operated by the developer. Cloudflare protects the connection. Blizzard's API is queried only to verify your own characters and fetch your own official profile data.

Your rights (GDPR / LGPD)

You can do all of the following at any time:

  • see, download and delete your data, and object to processing:
    • without installing anything, at forever.gameplaybr.com.br/me. Sign in with Battle.net to prove the characters are yours.
    • in game: Privacy › Delete my cloud data.
  • withdraw any consent: turn the layer off.

Deleting puts your characters on the exclusion list, so data that arrives about them later is discarded on arrival.

Security

  • Backups are encrypted end to end.
  • Character identifiers are stored as keyed codes, with the key held outside the database.
  • Data requests require participation and are rate-limited.
  • The admin console only shows aggregates, never individuals.

Changes

Material changes are announced in game and in the addon's changelog.
